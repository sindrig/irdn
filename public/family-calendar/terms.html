Personal household use

Family Calendar is operated by Sindri Guðmundsson for personal household use through an OpenClaw-hosted assistant. It is not a public sign-up service. These draft terms describe the authorized Calendar workflow; they do not grant or expand authorization.

Your authorization and control

Connect only accounts and calendars you are authorized to use. The owner selects the target family calendars and approves the sync scope. Initial preview approval establishes standing permission for routine creates and updates within that scope, without approval for each event. The sync does not delete events or change manually maintained events. Review the calendar's sharing settings before copying family information into it.

The owner can decline changes, stop the integration, revoke Google access and edit or delete calendar events directly. Connecting an account alone does not authorize calendar changes; changes must remain within the separately approved sync scope.

Check schedules and changes

Neither the assistant nor this integration guarantees calendar completeness, accuracy, availability or timely updates. Confirm important times, cancellations and locations with Abler or the relevant organizer. The integration does not replace official notices.
