Family Calendar

Draft for owner review — 18 October 2026. Not yet published or effective. These draft pages describe an authorized personal household integration; they do not confirm that an initial calendar import has completed.

Family Calendar is a personal household integration operated by Sindri Guðmundsson through an OpenClaw-hosted assistant. It is not a public sign-up service.

Calendar integration

The authorized workflow reads training schedules from Abler and creates or updates events in selected family Google Calendars within the owner's approved scope. The initial preview approval establishes standing permission for routine creates and updates within that scope, without approval for each event. The sync does not delete events or change manually maintained events. The owner controls the selected calendars and can change or stop the authorization.

Google access is limited to Calendar permissions and basic account identity. The integration does not request Gmail, Drive, Contacts or other Google Workspace product access.
