Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026.

Who operates this integration

Family Calendar is a personal household integration operated by Sindri Guðmundsson through an OpenClaw-hosted assistant. For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, contact sindrigudmundsson@gmail.com.

Data and permissions

The integration uses Google Calendar OAuth permissions and basic Google account identity, such as the connected account's email address. Calendar access may include calendar identifiers and event details such as titles, times, locations and descriptions needed to select the calendar, compare events and create or update approved entries. It does not request Gmail, Drive or Contacts access.

The assistant also reads Abler training schedules, which may contain family members' names, teams, training times and locations. Relevant training details may be copied into selected Google Calendars within the owner's approved sync scope.

How data is used and shared

Data is used to compare schedules and carry out calendar creates and updates within the owner's approved scope. Initial preview approval establishes standing permission for routine creates and updates within that scope; approval is not required for each routine event. The sync does not delete events or change manually maintained events. This policy does not grant or expand authorization.

Data is processed on the assistant's host and may be processed by the assistant and model providers configured by the owner when needed for the requested task. Google receives calendar data written to Google Calendar; people with access to that calendar may see it under its sharing settings. Abler is the source of training information. Configured hosting, assistant and model providers are third parties, not a claim that all processing stays on one device. The owner controls which hosting, assistant and model providers are configured and should review changes to that configuration.

Family Calendar does not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Storage, retention and control

Google OAuth tokens are stored encrypted by gog on the assistant's host. Calendar information, proposed changes and task records may also be present in assistant conversations, local files or logs, depending on the configuration. Encryption of tokens is not a guarantee that every copy of task data is encrypted or that unauthorized access is impossible.

The owner controls retention and deletion of assistant-held records and can stop the integration, remove local authorization and revoke access through Google Account connections. There is no fixed retention period established for this workflow. Deletion of local records may require separate cleanup of conversations, logs or backups and is subject to configured providers' retention practices.

Revoking access stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete calendar events already created or records already retained. Events can be removed from Google Calendar separately. Contact the operator for help reviewing or deleting assistant-held records.

Changes

Material changes to data use or providers should be reviewed by the owner before continued use. The last-updated date above identifies the latest revision of this policy.
